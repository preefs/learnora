Module 2: Document Preparation

Lesson Overview

Welcome to Module 2! In this section, you will learn standard practices for drafting, formatting, and structuring professional office documents such as business letters, memos, and reports.

Key Topics

  • Standard Business Letter Formatting: Block and modified block styles, margins, and spacing.
  • Internal Memorandums: Crafting clear, concise workplace memos with proper header fields (To, From, Date, Subject).
  • Proofreading & Editing: Catching grammar errors, ensuring tone professionalism, and verifying alignment.
