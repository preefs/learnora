Module 5: Office Productivity

Lesson Overview

Welcome to Module 5! In this final section, you will learn time management techniques, daily prioritization strategies, calendar scheduling, and tools to maximize daily workflow efficiency in an office environment.

Key Topics

  • Task Prioritization & Time Management: Using methods like the Eisenhower Matrix to categorize urgent vs. important tasks.
  • Calendar & Schedule Management: Managing meetings, appointments, reminders, and avoiding scheduling conflicts.
  • Workflow Automation & Tools: Leveraging office productivity suites and digital task managers to reduce manual repetition.
