Module 1: Keyboard & Typing Skills

Lesson Overview

Welcome to Module 1! In this section, you will learn the fundamentals of keyboard familiarity, proper hand positioning, typing accuracy, and how to build a basic typing speed essential for office environments.

Key Topics

  • Home Row Positioning: Keeping your fingers anchored on the ASDF and JKL; keys.
  • Accuracy vs. Speed: Prioritizing correct keystrokes before increasing words per minute (WPM).
  • Office Ergonomics: Maintaining proper posture and wrist alignment to prevent fatigue.
