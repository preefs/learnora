Module 4: Professional Communication

Lesson Overview

Welcome to Module 4! In this section, you will learn effective workplace communication strategies, professional email etiquette, telephone etiquette, and interpersonal conflict resolution in an office setting.

Key Topics

  • Business Email Etiquette: Writing clear subject lines, professional greetings, concise bodies, and proper sign-offs.
  • Telephone & Virtual Meeting Etiquette: Professional greeting protocols, active listening, and clear verbal communication.
  • Interpersonal Workplace Communication: Constructive feedback, professional tone, and collaborative problem-solving.
